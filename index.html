How to install

  1. Open the downloaded Setup and follow its steps.
  2. If Windows shows a SmartScreen warning, click More info, then Run anyway. The Setup does not have a digital signature yet.
  3. Approve the administrator prompt.

Installing a newer Setup replaces the older version. Your projects are kept.

What is new

From this version on, Studio tells you when an update is ready. You can also use Help, Check for updates.
